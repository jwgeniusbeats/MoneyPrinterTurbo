Terms of Service

What this is

Nognize ("we", "us") runs a psychology and mind-facts content channel across YouTube, TikTok, Instagram and Facebook. This page covers the terms for our internal automation tooling ("the app") that publishes our own video content to accounts we own and control (@nognize on each platform).

What the app does

  • Generates short-form video content on a schedule.
  • Publishes that content to our own accounts via each platform's official API (YouTube Data API, Meta Graph API, TikTok Content Posting API).
  • Reads back public performance metrics (views, likes, comments) for our own posts to improve future content.

The app does not act on behalf of, or access data belonging to, any account other than our own. It has no public-facing login, sign-up, or user accounts of its own.

Acceptable use

The app is used solely to operate the Nognize channel in compliance with each connected platform's developer terms and community guidelines.

Privacy Policy

Last updated September 15, 2026

Who this covers

This policy covers Nognize's internal automation tooling ("the app"), which publishes our own content to our own social accounts. It is not a consumer-facing app and does not collect data from the public through the app itself.

Data we handle

  • Video files and captions we create, uploaded to our own accounts on each platform.
  • OAuth access tokens for our own accounts (YouTube, Facebook, Instagram, TikTok), used only to publish and read our own content's public performance stats. Stored locally, not shared with any third party.
  • Public performance metrics (views, likes, comments) for our own posts, used to improve future content.

Separately, our public-facing landing page collects an email address only if a visitor voluntarily submits it to be notified about new content. That address is used only for that purpose and is never sold or shared.

Data we don't collect

The app does not collect data about people who watch our videos, does not access any other TikTok/YouTube/Meta account's data, and does not use tracking pixels or third-party analytics on our own accounts beyond each platform's own built-in insights.
