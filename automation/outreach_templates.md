# Cold Outreach Templates: Clip Service

Concept only. Nothing here gets sent anywhere automatically. Fiverr's
38,000+ competing gigs mean organic search traffic will be slow at first;
direct outreach to podcasters/streamers who clearly aren't repurposing
their content yet is the fastest way to a first review.

## Where to find targets
- Small-to-mid podcasts on Spotify/YouTube (look for ones with <5k
  subscribers posting full episodes but no Shorts/Reels/TikTok presence)
- Twitch streamers with VODs but no clips channel
- LinkedIn/YouTube webinar hosts, course creators, coaches doing live
  Q&As with no short-form presence

## Template 1: Twitter/X or Instagram DM (short, casual)
> Hey [name], been watching [podcast/stream name], the [specific
> moment/topic] episode was great. Noticed you're not clipping it into
> Shorts/Reels yet, I do exactly that (vertical cuts plus word-by-word
> captions). Want me to send you a free sample clip from your last
> episode, no strings attached?

## Template 2: Email (slightly longer, for podcast contact emails)
> Subject: Free short-form clip from [episode name]
>
> Hi [name],
>
> I turn podcast/stream episodes into vertical shorts for TikTok/Reels/
> YouTube Shorts: highlight cut, 9:16 crop, word-by-word captions.
>
> I picked one moment from [episode name] ([timestamp]) and cut a free
> sample so you can see the style before spending anything: [link once
> made].
>
> If it's useful, I do this for $10/clip or $50 for a 5-pack. If not, no
> worries at all, just wanted to share it.
>
> [name]

## Template 3: Reddit / community reply (never cold-post, only reply to relevant threads asking about clipping/repurposing help)
> I built a word-by-word caption plus auto-crop pipeline for exactly this.
> Happy to cut one clip from your content for free so you can judge the
> style yourself before paying for anything, no obligation.

## Template 4: follow-up after delivering a free sample

Send once you've delivered the free clip (Reddit DM, reply, or wherever the
conversation moved). Goal: convert a happy free-sample recipient into a paid
Fiverr order, specifically so it comes with a review.

> Here's the clip: [link]. No pressure at all, just wanted you to have
> something real to look at.
>
> If it's useful and you want more of these, I do this on Fiverr for $10 a
> clip: [gig link]. If you end up ordering, a review helps a lot since I'm
> still building up my rating on there.
>
> Either way, glad I could help.

## Notes on the free-sample approach
- A free first clip is the strongest way to counter zero reviews: it lets
  quality speak instead of asking someone to pay a stranger with no
  track record.
- Only offer this to a handful of targets at a time. Each one costs real
  editing time (Whisper + render), not free to produce at scale.
- Once 2-3 people accept and like the result, ask directly for a Fiverr
  order plus review even at a discounted/free rate, specifically to build
  the review count competitors already have.
