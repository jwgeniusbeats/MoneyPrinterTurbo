# Fiverr gig kit: copy-paste fields (2026-10-01)

Supersedes the shorter gig copy in `animation_service_offer.md`. Everything below
is checked against the limits Fiverr listed (title 80 chars, description 1,200,
5 tags of max 20 chars, package descriptions max 100). Source:
[eduearnhub](https://eduearnhub.com/?p=5875). NOT verified in Fiverr's own form: check
the limits when you paste, Fiverr may differ.

## 0. Before you start (Netherlands)
- Identity verification must be completed after creating the freelancer profile
  and before the first gig can be published
  ([Fiverr help NL](https://help.fiverr.com/hc/nl/articles/22582440968849-Persoonlijke-en-bedrijfsinformatie-verificatie)).
  Have your ID ready.
- Fee: flat 20% on every order, including extras and tips. New sellers wait 14 days
  after the buyer accepts before earnings clear
  ([vaultleap](https://vaultleap.com/blog/fiverr-fees-explained-2026),
  [Wise](https://wise.com/us/blog/how-to-get-paid-on-fiverr)). Payout via bank, PayPal,
  Payoneer or Wise.
- AI rules ([Fiverr](https://help.fiverr.com/hc/en-us/articles/37333301560593)): AI use is
  allowed if it is transparent and legal. If a buyer asks for non-AI work, you must
  honour it. Deliveries must be tailored to the buyer, not mass-produced. Voices that
  mimic a real person without consent are prohibited. The description below already
  says AI voices and templates are used.

I will create a hand drawn style animated explainer short with voiceover

## 4. Description (850/1200)
```
Need a short vertical explainer for Reels, TikTok or YouTube Shorts, but no animator and no agency budget?

I turn your message into a clean hand-drawn style animation: big headlines, step-by-step reveals, before/after comparisons, voice-over, soft music and word-by-word captions. Ready to upload in 1080x1920 MP4.

HOW IT WORKS
1. You send the idea or 3-5 bullet points (or just your topic and I draft the script for you to approve).
2. I build the animation and send you the finished video.
3. You get revisions as per your package.

VOICE: a natural AI voice, or your own voice if you send short recordings per scene.

HONEST NOTE: I use code-based animation templates and AI voices. Every script is written for you. No custom characters or logo animation (ask first).

Perfect for coaches, course creators, small SaaS, consultants and educators.
```

## 5. Packages (launch prices, see note)
| | Basic | Standard | Premium |
|---|---|---|---|
| Description (max 100) | 20 s video, 3 scenes, AI voice, captions, music. 1 revision. | 30-45 s video, 4-5 scenes, AI voice or your own, captions, music. 2 revisions. | Up to 60 s, script writing included, AI or own voice, captions, music. 3 revisions. |

| Revisions | 1 | 2 | 3 |
| Price | $60 | $120 | $200 |

Price note: market explainers for a 60 s video are listed at $500-2,000 for freelancers
([source](https://videoexplainers.com/blog/how-much-does-explainer-video-cost)), but with
zero reviews a buyer takes a risk. Low prices can also attract demanding buyers
([unilink](https://www.unilink.us/blog/how-to-make-money-on-fiverr-2026)). Raise prices
after the first 3 reviews. This is a judgement call, not tested.

## 6. Gig extras
- Script writing: +$20 (Basic and Standard)
- Fast delivery (24 h): +$25
- Extra 15 seconds: +$25

## 7. Questions to ask the buyer (requirements)
1. What is the topic and the one thing viewers should remember? (3-5 bullet points)
2. Who is the audience?
3. What should they do at the end (follow, book, visit link)?
4. Handle or text for the last screen (if any)?
5. Voice: AI voice (male/female) or your own recordings?
6. Colours or a music mood you like (calm, upbeat)? Music will be royalty-free or synthesised.

## 8. FAQ
- **Can I use my own voice?** Yes. Send one short recording per scene. I do not clone voices.
- **Do you use AI?** Yes: AI voices and code-based animation templates. The script and
  scenes are made for your topic. If you want fully non-AI work, tell me before ordering.
- **Can you animate my logo or custom characters?** Not in the standard packages. Ask first.
- **Which formats?** 1080x1920 MP4 (vertical). Other sizes on request.
- **How many revisions?** As per package. Changes to text and timing are included.

## 11. Rules for yourself
- Reply within an hour when possible (response time affects ranking).
- Deliver one day early. Ask for the review after delivery, never before.
- If the buyer's request does not fit the template (custom characters, long video),
  say so before they order.
